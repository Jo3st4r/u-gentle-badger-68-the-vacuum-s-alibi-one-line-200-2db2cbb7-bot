One excuse — a single line, 149 characters — from a robot vacuum
explaining why it missed a spot, built for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7).

## The excuse

> My lidar mapped the spot, my floor plan filed it under 'decorative', and my schedule moved on. The spot is now a feature of the room. You're welcome.

1 spot missed, 1 feature added, 0 apologies: the vacuum did not
fail to see the dirt — its lidar mapped it perfectly, its floor
plan reclassified it as decor, and its schedule moved on. A
machine detail (the lidar scan) with a surprising turn (the miss
was a filed decision, not an error), and a kicker that takes
credit for the dirt instead of apologising for it.

python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
